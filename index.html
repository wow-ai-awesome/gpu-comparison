Key Privacy Takeaways

  • Training on workloads: None of these providers train on your models/datasets/prompts by default. Modal and Together AI have the clearest written “will not train without consent” language. Together AI’s training is an explicit opt-in (off by default).
  • Account data: Email, billing, and usage metadata are used for service operation and analytics across all platforms.
  • Deletion: You are responsible for terminating pods/instances and deleting persistent volumes. Ephemeral storage is generally wiped on terminate (RunPod and Vast.ai emphasize they do not keep backups for privacy reasons).
  • Opt-out: Workload training is usually off by default (nothing to opt out of). Together AI is the main one where you actively enable Zero Data Retention to stop prompt storage.

Stronger Privacy Options

  • Self-hosted / local hardware — Zero provider access.
  • Confidential computing — Hardware isolation (NVIDIA Confidential Computing, Intel TDX, AMD SEV) so the operator cannot inspect memory/GPU contents. Available on Azure, Google Cloud, and specialized providers.
  • Private Cloud / single-tenant — Lambda Private Cloud or reserved clusters with contractual isolation and secure wipe.
